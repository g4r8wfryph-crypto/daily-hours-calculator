How to use Tap to expand ▾
  • Use 24-hour time. 4:30 in the afternoon is 16:30. There is no AM/PM anywhere in this tool.
  • You don't need to type the colon. Type 0745 and it becomes 07:45 when you leave the field. 9 becomes 09:00, 930 becomes 09:30. A field outlined in red means the time isn't valid.
  • Fill the rows top to bottom, in the order the day happened. Times past midnight are handled automatically.
  • Untick any segment you didn't run. A travel leg you leave blank drops out on its own — the work either side runs straight through it.
  • Tick "Lunch taken" only if you took one, then enter the clock-out and clock-in times for the break.
  • Travel pays after the first 0:30 of each leg — a 0:15 drive counts as 0:00, a 0:50 drive counts as 0:20.
  • Overtime request is separate from your real Clock out. Type the time you plan to leave and it shows the total and the overtime to ask for, rounded up to the next 0:15. If a travel leg has started but hasn't finished yet, it's counted as regular work so you get the maximum to request now.
